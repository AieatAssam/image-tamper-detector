# Round 10 — a real AI-generation corpus from open sources

Round 9 fetches ELSA1M. This round adds the two datasets the forensics
community actually uses to evaluate diffusion detectors, both open access with
no authentication.

## Why not Kaggle

Checked on 2026-08-30: no `~/.kaggle/kaggle.json`, no `kaggle` CLI, no `KAGGLE_*`
environment variables on this machine. Every Kaggle dataset is therefore Tier B
under decision D7 — it needs a manual credential step and cannot be fetched
unattended.

Separately, the most-cited Kaggle option is unsuitable on technical grounds.
**CIFAKE** (120k images, SD 1.4 vs CIFAR-10) is **32x32 pixels**. Every detector
in this project needs real resolution: JPEG 8x8 block structure, CFA Bayer
periodicity, SIFT keypoints, spectral peak lattices. At 32x32 all of them are
meaningless. CIFAKE is built for CNN classifiers, not forensics. Do not use it,
and record the reason so it is not proposed again.

Higher-resolution Kaggle sets exist ("AI vs Human-Generated Images";
"ai-generated-images-vs-real-images", 12k from SD/MJ/DALL-E) but they still need
credentials and have weaker provenance than the two below. If the repo owner
later supplies `kaggle.json`, they become viable as an additional axis.

## S1 — SD3.5 + Flux (DO THIS FIRST: smaller, fully open, current generators)

Zenodo DOI **10.5281/zenodo.22166280**, published 2026-08-29.
License **CC-BY-4.0** — fully open, no non-commercial clause.
Single file `datasets.zip`, **3.58 GB**.
Direct: https://zenodo.org/api/records/22166280/files/datasets.zip/content

1000 images from **Stable Diffusion 3.5** and **Flux-schnell**, generated using
the Synthbuster prompt set. These are 2025/26-era generators. Everything else we
have — including ELSA — is SD 1.x-era, so this is the only data that tests
whether our detectors work against what people are actually generating with now.

Do this one first: it is a third the size, the licence is unencumbered, and it
answers the most important question.

## S2 — Synthbuster (the forensics benchmark, 9 generators)

Bammey, "Synthbuster: Towards Detection of Diffusion Model Generated Images",
IEEE Open Journal of Signal Processing, 2023.
Zenodo DOI **10.5281/zenodo.10066460**. License **CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0**.
Single file `synthbuster.zip`, **12.37 GB**.
Direct: https://zenodo.org/api/records/10066460/files/synthbuster.zip/content

1000 images from each of **9 generators**: DALL-E 2, DALL-E 3, Adobe Firefly,
Midjourney v5, Stable Diffusion 1.3, 1.4, 2 and XL, and Glide.

The property that makes this the right dataset: the images were generated from
descriptions of **RAISE-1k** photographs (Dang-Nguyen et al., ACM MMSys 2015), a
raw-camera forensics dataset. So the real counterparts are genuine unmodified
camera originals — which is exactly what `cfa` and `prnu` have never had enough
of, and it gives real/generated pairing for `source_balance_rule`.

**Licence note, and it binds:** CC-BY-**NC**-SA. Non-commercial use only, and
share-alike on derivatives. Research evaluation is fine. Record the clause
explicitly in `docs/corpus.md` and the manifest — it is a stricter term than
anything else in the corpus, and a future reader must not assume the whole
corpus is CC-BY. Under D7b nothing is redistributed anyway: bytes stay
gitignored, only the manifest and citation are committed.

## Constraints

- **Disk**: 117 GB free at time of writing; ~16 GB of downloads plus extraction.
  Fetch S1 first, verify the pipeline end to end, then S2. Do not fetch both
  before either works.
- **Sampling**: do NOT ingest 9000+ images. Per the round 6b precedent and the
  measured ~2.2 s/image cost, draw a fixed-seed stratified sample — target
  30-60 images per generator, so roughly 300-600 total — and record the
  selection in the manifest so it reproduces without re-downloading. Use the
  `--sample` fast path added in round 8b.
- **Axis naming**: these are a new axis, not `real_ai` top-ups. Suggest
  `synthbuster` and `sd35_flux`, each row carrying its `generator` field taken
  from the dataset's own directory or metadata — never inferred.
- **D7b**: `git check-ignore -v` on the download directory BEFORE fetching.
  Manifest and citations committed; image bytes never.
- **Balance**: the corpus is currently 400 IMD2020 classical manipulations
  against 12 AI images. After this round it should be roughly comparable
  between the classical and AI axes. Report the final counts per axis; if the
  AI axis still cannot be balanced against IMD2020, say so, because that
  imbalance is what has made AI detection unmeasurable.

## What this round is for

Not a better fused AUC. The point is that AI-generation detection becomes
MEASURABLE for the first time, on current generators, with per-generator
breakdown.

Report per-generator AUC for every AI-capable detector — `spectral`, `entropy`
(or its MLEP replacement), `cfa`, `npr`, `aeroblade`, `learned`, `clip_probe` if
it landed. A detector that works on SD 1.4 and fails on Flux is the single most
useful thing this corpus can tell us, and pooling generators would hide it
exactly as pooling corpora hid the splicebuster result in round 8.
