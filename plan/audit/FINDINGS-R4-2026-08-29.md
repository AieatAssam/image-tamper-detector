## I1 — BLOCKING — `copy_move` misses the repository's own reference forgery

`data/samples/tampered/landscape_copy_paste.jpg` was generated by this repo
(`scripts/create_tampered_sample.py`) with known parameters: a region of
15% of width and height copied from (70% W, 20% H) to (30% W, 20% H), circular
mask, Gaussian feather sigma=11, saved at JPEG quality 92, image resized to
1920x1080 before the paste.

Current behaviour:
```
FUSED: likely_authentic  score 0.283
copy_move  flagged=False  score=0.000  threshold=1.000
reason: "no_forgery_found: keypoints were sufficient but no verified affine cluster was found"
```

This is NOT the `insufficient_keypoints` path. The detector ran, found enough
keypoints, and concluded clean on the exact forgery class it exists to catch.

Consequence: `copy_move`'s 0.606 within-source AUC is separating something other
than copy-move forgery, and it holds a 0.099 fusion weight on that basis.
Note also `threshold=1.000` against `score=0.000` — a degenerate operating point.

INSTRUMENT BEFORE YOU TUNE. Print, for this one image, at each stage:
  1. keypoint count after the downscale
  2. surviving match count after the k=3 self-match and the Lowe ratio test
  3. surviving match count after the `min_offset` filter
  4. the (dx,dy) offset histogram, and the size of the largest cluster
  5. the RANSAC inlier count for each cluster
Then say which stage kills it. The plausible culprits, in order:
  - the paste offset is 40% of image width at full resolution, but the detector
    downscales the longest side to 1600 while the sample is 1920x1080 — check
    whether `min_offset=32px` is being applied in downscaled or original
    coordinates, and whether the true offset survives
  - the 8px offset-cluster grid may split one genuine cluster across adjacent
    bins, leaving each below the 8-member floor
  - Gaussian feathering at sigma=11 plus quality-92 JPEG degrades SIFT
    descriptors in the pasted region; the ratio test at 0.75 may be too strict
  - the source region is sky/mountain in a landscape — low texture — so this may
    partly be the documented textureless failure mode, in which case the honest
    fix is that the detector reports its uncertainty rather than "no forgery"

Whatever you find, do NOT loosen a parameter until the corpus copy_move family
still passes; report the before/after on BOTH this sample and the family.

## I2 — HIGH — Fitted weights are ANTI-CORRELATED with detector skill

This is the root cause of what round 3 reported as "fusion loses to its best
member" and marked S10 failed. It is a fit defect, not an inevitability.

From `backend/app/analysis/calibration.json`, sorted by weight:

| detector | weight | within_source_auc | heldout_auc |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| prnu | **0.190** | 0.502 | 0.520 |
| spectral | **0.164** | 0.559 | **0.467** |
| double_jpeg | 0.140 | 0.663 | **0.893** |
| copy_move | 0.099 | 0.594 | 0.633 |
| jpeg_ghosts | 0.085 | 0.552 | 0.693 |
| exif | 0.036 | 0.508 | **0.367** |

The two largest weights go to a chance-level detector (`prnu`, 0.520) and a
BELOW-chance one (`spectral`, 0.467). The strongest detector by a wide margin
(`double_jpeg`, 0.893) ranks third. `exif` at 0.367 — well below chance — still
carries positive weight. Fused 0.8267 losing to `double_jpeg` alone at 0.8933
follows directly.

Two things to investigate, and say which you found:
  (a) The zero-weight guard is applied to `within_source_auc`, which is
      compressed toward 0.5 for every detector, while the ranking disagreement
      shows up in `heldout_auc`. A guard on the less discriminative of two
      available metrics will not fire when it should — `spectral` at
      within 0.559 passes while its heldout is 0.467.
  (b) Feature scaling. If `logit(score)` inputs have very different variances
      across detectors, an L2 penalty shrinks the large-variance features
      hardest regardless of their usefulness, which produces exactly this
      inversion. Standardise the features before fitting, or penalise on the
      standardised scale.

Fix direction: the fit must not rank a chance-level detector above a 0.89 one.
After refitting, assert as a gate that Spearman rank correlation between weight
and held-out skill is positive across detectors with a defined AUC. Do NOT fix
this by hand-setting weights — that is explicitly forbidden by S10.

## I2b — the below-chance guard needs a confidence interval, not a point estimate

`prnu` has `within_source_auc` 0.5019 and passes a `<= 0.5` guard by 0.0019.
A point estimate cannot distinguish that from chance. Replace the guard with:
drop a detector to weight 0 unless its AUC exceeds 0.5 by more than one
standard error, using the Hanley–McNeil standard error for AUC:

    SE = sqrt( (A(1-A) + (n_pos-1)(Q1 - A^2) + (n_neg-1)(Q2 - A^2)) / (n_pos*n_neg) )
    where Q1 = A/(2-A) and Q2 = 2A^2/(1+A)

Record the AUC, its SE, and the drop decision per detector in
`calibration.json`, and document the rule in `docs/calibration.md`.
