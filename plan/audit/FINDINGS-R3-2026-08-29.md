# Round 3 — the source-identity confound

Round 2 did good work: Python 3.14.7 is now consistent across venv, Dockerfile
and CI; negative weights are gone; `spectral`, `cfa` and `prnu` carry real
weight again; 54 tests pass; the 30 mislabelled `authentic` rows are gone; and
G4/G6 were correctly reported `partially-fixed` rather than overclaimed.

Then a measurement invalidated most of the conclusions drawn from the corpus.

---

## H1 — CRITICAL — Detector ranking is measuring SOURCE IDENTITY, not manipulation

The G1 repair made every `authentic` row derive from `landscape_original.jpg`,
while `manipulated` rows come from four different sources. Source identity is
therefore almost perfectly correlated with the label, and any detector that can
tell a receipt from a landscape scores well without detecting anything.

Recomputing each AUC with the source image held constant (landscape_original
only, where both classes come from the same picture):

| detector | pooled AUC | within-source AUC | fitted weight | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| double_jpeg | 0.307 | **0.781** | **0.000** | the strongest true signal, weighted at zero |
| spectral | 0.779 | 0.700 | 0.147 | genuinely good, survives the control |
| jpeg_ghosts | 0.473 | 0.588 | 0.087 | real but weak |
| copy_move | 0.733 | 0.559 | 0.209 | much weaker than it looks |
| ela | 0.266 | 0.480 | 0.000 | ~chance, not inverted |
| entropy | 0.313 | 0.440 | 0.000 | ~chance, not inverted |
| exif / qtable | 0.500 | 0.500 | 0.120 / 0.000 | constant |
| **cfa** | 0.646 | **0.385** | **0.186** | BELOW chance once controlled, yet weighted |
| **prnu** | 0.652 | **0.365** | **0.147** | BELOW chance once controlled, yet weighted |

Two consequences, both serious:

1. `cfa` (0.186) and `prnu` (0.147) carry real fusion weight while being
   **below chance** on the only comparison that controls for the confound. The
   fusion is being steered by two detectors that are worse than a coin.
2. `double_jpeg` at **0.781** within-source is the best processing-history
   detector in the project and contributes **nothing**. Round 1's sign fix was
   correct; the pooled 0.307 that appeared to undo it is the confound, not a
   regression. Do not revert that fix.

## H2 — PLAN AMENDED — the corpus must balance sources; this needs the real camera axis

`plan/stages/S05-corpus-and-benchmark.yaml` now carries a
`source_balance_rule`: every source image that contributes any entry must
contribute BOTH classes, and no source may supply more than 40% of either.

Only a genuinely authentic source may produce authentic rows, and
`landscape_original.jpg` is the only authentic sample in `data/samples`.
So this rule **cannot be satisfied without the real_camera axis**. The real
corpus is now a hard prerequisite for a meaningful calibration, not an extra.

The rule also requires `benchmark.py` to report a `within_source_auc` per
detector, and S10 to fit and gate on THAT rather than the pooled AUC.

## H3 — PLAN AMENDED — the real_camera criterion was too strict; it is my defect

Round 2 rejected 10 of 10 otherwise-qualifying Wikimedia camera candidates
because EXIF `PixelXDimension` (0xA002) was absent, ending with **zero**
real_camera entries. That tag is simply missing from a large fraction of genuine
camera JPEGs. The criterion was mine and it was wrong: rejecting every candidate
is a worse outcome than admitting a weaker, clearly-labelled grade of evidence.

`plan/stages/S05-corpus-and-benchmark.yaml` `real_camera.criteria` now accepts
two grades, and every entry must record which it met in a required
`unresized_evidence` field:

- `strict`  — 0xA002 present and equal to the decoded width
- `relaxed` — 0xA002 absent, no editor `Software` tag (0x0131), and decoded
              dimensions plausible for the recorded Model

What keeps this honest: **`cfa` MUST consume only `strict` entries and MUST
report NOT_APPLICABLE for `relaxed` ones.** CFA structure does not survive
resizing, so a relaxed entry cannot validate it. Enforce that in code, not in a
comment.

Round 2 also hit Wikimedia HTTP 429 with `Retry-After: 600`. Respect it: back
off, cache what you have already downloaded, and fetch in batches. Partial
progress recorded honestly is the expected outcome if the budget runs out.

## H4 — HIGH — `exif` is weighted 0.120 while returning a constant 0.500

`exif` scores exactly 0.500 both pooled and within-source, i.e. its output does
not vary across the corpus, yet it carries the third-largest fusion weight.
A constant cannot contribute information; the weight is fitting noise.

Either the generated EXIF is uniform across the corpus (so the detector is
correct and the corpus is at fault — vary Software tags, thumbnails, and
datetime fields across generated entries), or the detector is collapsing its
five checks into a constant. Diagnose which before changing anything, and give
a constant-output detector zero weight either way.

`qtable` is the same shape at 0.500 and was correctly given weight 0.000;
round 2 already established its statistic is constant because the corpus uses
one libjpeg table. Varying the encoder across generated entries would fix both.

## H5 — MEDIUM — a below-chance detector must not receive positive weight

`jpeg_ghosts` is 0.473 pooled and `cfa`/`prnu` are below chance within-source,
yet all three hold positive weights. Round 2 correctly removed NEGATIVE weights;
the complementary guard is missing. Add an explicit rule to `scripts/calibrate.py`:
a detector whose **within-source** AUC is at or below 0.5 gets weight 0 and a
recorded reason, rather than being fitted. State the rule in
`docs/calibration.md`.
